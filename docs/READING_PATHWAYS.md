# PHENOMENA · Phenomenological Library and Reading Pathways

## Editorial scope
**56 study-guide book entries · 355 original guided lessons · 223 lexicon entries · 85 seminars · 850 semantic audio chapters.**

These are original **thematic study companions**. They do not contain complete copies of the primary works, licensed English translations or audiobook recordings. Many older lessons are interpretive summaries; newly added lessons are intentionally NOT assigned unverified paragraph numbers.

### Auditable narration length

**521,319 script words** across all seminar chapters. At 115 wpm, **75.55 h**; at 125 wpm, **69.51 h**; at 130 wpm, **66.84 h**. These figures estimate narrated *script duration* and count deliberately repeated conceptual practice. Real TTS pauses, speech rate and languages vary.

### Recommended progression

1. **Access and method:** Husserl, *The Idea of Phenomenology*, *Logical Investigations*, and *Ideas I*.
2. **Subjectivity and temporality:** *Cartesian Meditations*, the time-consciousness lectures, *Passive Synthesis*, and *Crisis*.
3. **Existence and ontological difference:** Heidegger, *Being and Time*, *History of the Concept of Time*, *Basic Problems of Phenomenology*.
4. **Realism and social life:** Reinach, Stein, Scheler, Ingarden, Schutz, and Fink.
5. **Embodiment and freedom:** Merleau-Ponty, Sartre, and Beauvoir.
6. **Alterity and ethics:** Levinas, Patočka, Waldenfels and Ricoeur.
7. **Late and critical movements:** Henry, Marion, Derrida, Ahmed, Ihde and contemporary enactive/clinical accounts.

## What the time estimate does and does not mean

The longer seminars are scaffolded spirals. They deliberately reintroduce key distinctions, ask listeners to revisit a concrete example, examine rival readings, and practice counterexamples. As a consequence, the word count is **not** the number of unique philosophical propositions or nonrepetitive research findings. Work-specific interpretive assertions are editorial drafts until checked against original-language critical editions.

No primary-text audiobooks are synthesized, no copyrighted primary translations are redistributed, and no APK is built by these editorial scripts.
